For partners

Auto Tuckshop, for funders, partners and press.

Auto Tuckshop records tuck shop sales on WhatsApp. This page is for people thinking about the product from the outside: what it's building towards, who's building it, and how to reach us.

The thesis

Sales records today. Financial access tomorrow.

A sale recorded on WhatsApp becomes part of a shop's financial history. Over time, that history is what could open the door to credit or supplier finance, something most informal shops have no path to today.

Month 1 Daily sales records

Voice and text messages become confirmed transactions.

Month 6 A clearer picture of the shop

Patterns emerge across sales, products and busy days.

Future, if owners choose Financial access

Those records may one day help a shop apply for credit or supplier finance. We don't share them with lenders today.

Why we built this

Built close to the problem.

Madrena grew up in rural Chipinge, ran a small tuck shop herself, taught herself programming, and now ships production code for the same kind of shopkeeping reality Auto Tuckshop serves.

Built by AakiTech.

Auto Tuckshop is built by AakiTech, a Zimbabwean team building practical software for African small businesses.
