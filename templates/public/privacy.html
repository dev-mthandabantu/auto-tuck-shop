Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

Auto Tuck Shop is a sales recording tool for small shops in Zimbabwe. Your sales records are your business — this policy explains plainly what we collect, who can see it, and what we do with it.

Your records, your tool

The sales you record through Auto Tuck Shop are a personal record-keeping tool — the same as writing in a book, just faster. Records are entered by you, in your own words, and exist to help you understand your own business. We do not share them with anyone outside the team running this service.

We are not connected to any payment system, point-of-sale device, or external reporting system. Sales records on Auto Tuck Shop are self-reported and not verified against any outside source — they reflect what you chose to enter, nothing more.

What we collect

To run the service, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and shop name
  • Sales messages and voice notes you send to the bot
  • Product names, quantities, and prices from those messages
  • Language preferences and setup choices
  • Technical records needed to keep the service running reliably

We do not collect your personal name, identity documents, or any financial account information. Your shop name is whatever you choose to call it — we never ask who you are as a person. The only thing that connects your records to you is your phone number, the same as any other WhatsApp conversation.

Who can see your data

Your sales records are visible to:

  • You — through your daily reports and any summaries you request from the bot
  • Auto Tuck Shop staff — only for the purpose of running and supporting the service

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or pass it to any third party for their own purposes.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the service: WhatsApp messaging infrastructure (Meta), cloud hosting, AI message parsing, voice transcription, media storage, and error monitoring. Each provider handles only the data they need to do their job and is not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

Data retention

We keep your shop's sales records for as long as you are using the service and for a reasonable period afterwards, in case you need historical data. You can request deletion at any time — see the Data Deletion page for how to do that.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about your shop, to correct it if it is wrong, and to request that we delete it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the support channel provided to your shop.

Contact

For any privacy questions, reach the Auto Tuck Shop team through the support channel provided when your shop was set up.
