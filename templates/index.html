How it works

Built for a busy counter, not a quiet office.

Each sale starts as an ordinary WhatsApp message. Auto Tuck Shop turns it into a confirmed record and sends back the numbers the shop can use.

1

Send what was sold

Type it, say it as a voice note, or mix Shona and English the way you normally speak.

2

Check the confirmation

The bot replies with items, quantities and totals before the sale becomes a confirmed record.

3

Use the report

Ask for today, this week or this month, and get a clear summary back on WhatsApp.

Trust

Your records are for running your shop.

Shop owners have asked directly about privacy, ZIMRA and who can see their numbers. The answer has to be plain.

Not FDMS. Not connected to ZIMRA.

Auto Tuck Shop is not a fiscalisation system and does not report sales to ZIMRA in real time.

We do not proactively share records

Your sales records are not sold, advertised with, or sent to tax authorities as a routine feed.

Like a notebook, but easier

Records are self-reported by the shop. They reflect what was typed or said, not bank or POS data.

Support access is limited

Auto Tuck Shop staff can see records only to run, debug and support the service.

You can ask us to delete it

Owners can request deletion of shop data connected to their phone number or shop.

The bigger story

Every recorded sale is a row in a financial history that did not exist before.

Informal retailers are economically active, but many remain invisible to lenders, insurers and supplier-credit systems. Auto Tuck Shop starts with the simplest wedge: help the owner remember what happened today.

Voice and text messages become confirmed transactions.
