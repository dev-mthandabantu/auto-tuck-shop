How it works

Built for a busy counter, not a quiet office.

Each sale starts as an ordinary WhatsApp message. Auto Tuckshop turns it into a confirmed record and sends back the numbers the shop can use.

1

Send what was sold

Type it, say it as a voice note, or mix Shona and English the way you normally speak.

2

Check the confirmation

The bot replies with items, quantities and totals before the sale becomes a confirmed record.

3

Use the report

Ask for today, this week or this month, and get a clear summary back on WhatsApp. Tell the bot what you pay for your stock and it shows your profit too.

Trust

Your records are for running your shop.

Not connected to ZIMRA.

Auto Tuckshop does not report sales to ZIMRA.

We don't sell your sales records or use them for advertising

Used only to run and support the service.

Like a notebook, but easier

Records are self-reported by the shop. They reflect what was typed or said, not bank or card-machine data.

Who can see your records

Your shop can see its reports. Our team accesses records when needed to run and support the service.

You can ask us to delete it

Owners can request deletion of shop data connected to their phone number or shop.
